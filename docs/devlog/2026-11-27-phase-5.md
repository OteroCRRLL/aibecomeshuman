# Dev log: 2026-11-27, phase 5 (shadow mode)

## What was done
- Live frame sources: OBS WebSocket screenshots of the game capture source (no extra
  software), a whole monitor, or a recording played in real time (testing without the
  game).
- `aibh shadow` and the **Live** tab: the AI watches, suggests every choice with its
  reason, and compares with what the operator picked. Stop saves the scene's memory; the
  end-of-chapter flowchart closes a scene automatically.
- The character's runner gained a live API (`perceive`, `suggest`, `record_pick`,
  `finish`), also used by scripted scenes now.
- In shadow mode memory is kept but the personality does not learn from the operator's
  picks (they were not the agent's).

## First real-time test (recorded negotiation from 07:50, Claude Haiku)
- All 9 menus caught; a suggestion about 4 s after each menu appeared on screen
  (reading the menu ~2 s + deciding ~2 s); one took 9 s and fell back to the rule.
- The operator's pick: first version relied on the highlight and mostly failed; now the
  protagonist's next line decides (shared words, then a cheap text guess): right in 6 of
  the 7 menus checked by hand.
- Haiku-Connor agreed with the operator 3 times out of 9: calmer and more "realistic",
  and chose a compromise instead of the sacrifice.
- ~$0.12 for 4 minutes of play.
