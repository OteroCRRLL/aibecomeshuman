# Dev log: 2026-11-26, phase 2 (memory and personality)

## What was done
- **Long-term memory per character**: recollections, known people/places/things with
  feelings and trust, promises with their status, and a versioned personality.
- **End of every scene**: consolidation (summarizer) and reflection (reflector), with
  trait changes bounded in code (±1 per trait per scene, 0–10, reason required).
- **Fixed prompt budget**: recent + important recollections, entities present in the
  scene, active promises, current personality.
- **Campaigns** keep a playthrough's memory across sessions; **chains** play several
  scenes in a row; steps can depend on earlier choices.
- Starting traits moved to the character sheets' front matter.
- **Control panel**: chains and campaign name in Play; new Memory tab (trait bars against
  their starting value, change history with reasons, recollections, people, promises).
- New invented chain for Markus ("A week with Markus", 4 scenes) to test memory,
  consequences and promises.

## First real run (Claude Haiku in every role)
- The whole 4-scene chain costs about **$0.01**.
- Memory carried over: Markus told the painter the truth about being late (citing his
  recollection), was recognized at the market by the woman he helped, recorded a
  commitment, and on Thursday remembered it without being reminded.
- Personality drifted slowly and with concrete reasons, e.g. obedience 7 → 6.5 after
  ignoring the drone, empathy 4 → 5 over the week, self-curiosity 3 → 4 after "I don't know".
- Tuned after the first run: the summarizer translated speaker names (breaking entity
  matching) and recorded feelings about people who were not in the scene. Fixed in its prompt.

## Known limitations
- The summarizer rates almost every scene 3/5 in importance.
- Haiku as reflector sometimes gives weak reasons (e.g. lowering obedience after
  obeying). The video profile uses Opus for reflection; to be compared before READY.
